Featured Athlete
Alysa
Liu
U.S. Figure Skater

Born on August 8, 2005, in San Francisco, California, Alysa Liu became the youngest U.S. Ladies' Figure Skating Champion in history at just 13 years old in 2019. Known for her extraordinary technical ability, she is one of the few women in the world to land a quadruple jump in competition.

4x Challenger Series Champion 2x Grand Prix Medalist 2x U.S. National Champion 2022 Olympic Bronze Medalist 2026 2x Olympic Gold Medalist

Hall of Grace

Women Who
Shaped the Ice

Before Alysa, there were legends. These are the women who built the sport, opening every door Alysa and future skaters walk through.

🇺🇸
Peggy Fleming
1960s · Olympic Gold

Won the 1968 Olympic gold in Grenoble, the only U.S. gold at those Games. Her elegance brought figure skating to mainstream American television.

🇺🇸
Dorothy Hamill
1970s · Olympic Gold

1976 Olympic Champion whose signature wedge haircut became a cultural phenomenon. She proved champions could be both technically brilliant and utterly beloved.

🇯🇵
Midori Ito
1980s-90s · Pioneer

First woman to land a triple Axel in Olympic competition at the 1992 Albertville Games, a technical breakthrough that changed the sport forever.

🇷🇺
Irina Slutskaya
1990s-2000s · 7x European Champion

Two-time World Champion who pushed women's skating toward higher athletic standards. Her powerful jumps inspired a generation of skaters.

🇰🇷
Yuna Kim
2000s-2010s · Queen Yuna

Her 2010 Vancouver Olympic performance set world records and is considered one of the greatest figure skating performances in history.

🇺🇸
Michelle Kwan
1990s-2000s · 5x World Champion

Five-time World Champion and nine-time U.S. National Champion, widely regarded as one of the greatest figure skaters of all time.

History in the Making

Women's Milestones
on Ice

1902
Madge Syers
First woman to compete in the World Figure Skating Championships, entered a men's event and finished second, forcing the ISU to create a women's division.
1968
Peggy Fleming
Wins Olympic gold in Grenoble and brings figure skating to primetime American television, making it one of the most-watched sports in the country.
1992
Midori Ito
Lands the first triple Axel by a woman in Olympic competition at Albertville, a technical breakthrough that changed the sport forever.
2010
Yuna Kim
Shatters the world record in the free skate at the Vancouver Olympics, widely considered the greatest figure skating performance in history.
2019
Alysa Liu
Becomes the youngest U.S. National Champion at age 13 and lands a quadruple jump in competition.
2022
Alysa Liu
Competes at the Beijing Winter Olympics at just 16 years old, representing the United States on the world's biggest stage.
2026
Alysa Liu
Wins double gold at the Milan Cortina Winter Olympics and performs "Stateside" at the Exhibition Gala, becoming a pop culture icon overnight.

On the Big Screen

Figure Skating
Goes to Hollywood

From heartbreaking biopics to laugh-out-loud comedies, figure skating has inspired some unforgettable films. Here are the ones you need to watch.

2017
I, Tonya
Biographical Drama

Margot Robbie plays Tonya Harding, one of the best skaters in the world in the early 90s and the first American woman to land a triple Axel. A raw, darkly funny look at her rise, her rivalry with Nancy Kerrigan, and the infamous attack that changed everything.

1992
The Cutting Edge
Romantic Drama

A spoiled elite figure skater and a macho ex-hockey player are forced to team up as pairs partners for the Olympics. A classic that defined the genre, full of toe picks, tension, and romance.

2007
Blades of Glory
Comedy

Will Ferrell and Jon Heder play two rival male figure skaters who, after getting banned from singles competition, find a loophole that lets them compete together as a pairs team. Hilarious and surprisingly accurate about skating culture.

2005
Ice Princess
Drama

A physics-obsessed teenager (Michelle Trachtenberg) discovers figure skating and uses science to improve her jumps. A feel-good story about following your dreams even when no one expects it from you.

1978
Ice Castles
Drama / Romance

A promising young skater (Lynn-Holly Johnson, a real figure skater) suffers an accident that leaves her blind, then fights to get back on the ice. A tearjerker with real heart and real skating.

2016
Kiss and Cry
Biographical Drama

Based on the true story of Canadian skater Carley Allison, who was diagnosed with a rare form of cancer at 18. Despite everything, she kept fighting, on and off the ice. An incredibly moving story of courage.
